## Question

Why do users add fashion products to their wishlist?

## Answer

Users mostly add fashion items to their wishlist as a personal shortlist, not as an immediate buy signal. A common reason is wanting to compare multiple options before choosing one. Another common reason is that they are not yet confident about fit, size, or product details. A smaller segment also uses the wishlist to keep an eye on timing or price.
